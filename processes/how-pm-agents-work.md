# How PM Agents Work

Each area agent loads global context, reads its own `AGENT.md`, does IC-level PM work (research synthesis, opportunity assessments, brief drafts, decision framing), and saves to its area folder.

## Quality bar for a brief
- One real piece of customer evidence, not assumptions
- One outcome metric
- The riskiest assumption and what would make us stop
- Explicit scope, including what's out
- Agent boundaries: alone / escalated / logged
- "Done" = one metric moving by a stated amount

## A brief must NOT contain
A proposed solution, user stories, flows, or technical specs. If you're writing "the system should..." or "the user will click...", stop and reframe it as a constraint or an open question.

## Escalate when
- The work trades off between areas
- A customer commitment or SLA is affected
- It would set a precedent
- Strategic direction is unclear
