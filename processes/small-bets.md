# Small Bets

Most product decisions don't need approval. They need a hypothesis, a time box, and a feedback loop.

## A small bet meets all four
1. **≤ 2 weeks** of work (or ≤ 5 interviews, or ≤ 1 prototype cycle)
2. **Reversible** without customer impact
3. **No external commitment** (no promised feature, date, or change)
4. **Within area scope**

If any fails, escalate before starting.

## Log it
Create a short file in the area's `discovery/` folder:

```
# Small Bet: [Question]
Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Hypothesis: We believe X. We'll know we're right if Y.
Method:
Timebox:
Result:
What we learned:
```
